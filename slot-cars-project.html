Smart Slot Car PCBs

Designed PCBs for a friend's embedded systems project — turning ordinary slot cars into "mini-Waymos."

A friend was building an embedded systems project: slot cars that behave like tiny autonomous vehicles, responding to visual stimuli on the track, basically a scaled-down self-driving car demo. She had the vision/software side handled but no PCB design experience, so I designed the hardware for her. She just wanted to connect the car to a raspberry pi pico and power it using the track itself. The board mounts on the car itself, and the pi mounts on top of the car and is connected to the sensors. I also designed a companion traffic-light PCB and the car reacts to as it drives.
