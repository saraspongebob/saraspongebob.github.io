Teaching ENG 1: Autonomous Rovers

Taught digital systems, circuit design, and embedded programming to classes of 20 students building their own autonomous rovers.

As a UCLA Academic Group Tutor for ENG 1: Autonomous Rovers, I taught digital systems concepts, circuit wiring, embedded programming (Arduino/C++), and schematic capture — folding real electrical design analysis into every lesson rather than treating it as an abstract intro course.

Beyond lecturing, I mentored students through the full design-build-test loop on their own rover PCBs: pushing them to think about design for manufacturability, not just "does it work once," and getting them comfortable with iterative testing when their first version inevitably didn't. Watching students go from not knowing what a schematic was to debugging their own rover's wiring by the end of the quarter was the most rewarding part of it.
